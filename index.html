How Cookie Crawl Works

🛷

1. Send Helpers

Pick a helper and send them out into the snowy woods, cozy kitchens, and frozen markets.

🥕

2. Gather Ingredients

They return with flour, sugar, chocolate chips, candy canes, and rare holiday spices.

🍪

3. Bake & Rank

Turn ingredients into cookies. The more you bake, the higher you climb on the live leaderboard.
